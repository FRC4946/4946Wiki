The 2015 [[FIRST Robotics Competition]] season, Recycle Rush, was the team's second season competing.

# The Game
In Recycle Rush, teams stacked totes from the loading station and landfill, put Recycling Containers and litter on top of the stacks, and moved them to the scoring platforms to earn points.  This year was different from most as teams didn't rank based on wins or RP, but instead would be ranked based on their average score.

# The Robot
![[DSC02341.jpg|600]]

The team's robot for that season was named Elder Moeller, after one of the graduated students from the [[2014 Aerial Assist]] season.  This robot used an H-drive with 5 omni wheels to allow the robot to move in any direction.  The robot also had two prongs and an elevator to grasp and stack totes both from the loading station and from the landfill.

# Competitions
All match data from [The Blue Alliance](https://www.thebluealliance.com/)

## Greater Toronto Central Regional

The team's first event of the 2015 season was at the Greater Toronto Central competition.  The team had a qualifying average score of 73.60, ranking 3rd.  The team captained alliance 3, and was eliminated in the quarterfinals.  The team won the [[Innovation in Control Award]].
![](https://youtu.be/AWXJDPgkIi0)
## North Bay Regional

The team then competed at the North Bay competition.  The team had a qualifying average score of 70.18, ranking 16th at the event.  The team was 1st pick of alliance 8, and was eliminated in the quarterfinals.  The team also won the [[Judges Award]].
