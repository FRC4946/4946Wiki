The 2016 [[FIRST Robotics Competition]] season, Stronghold, was the team's third season competing.

# The Game
In Stronghold, teams crossed defenses placed by the opposing alliance to access their keep, and then attempted to score boulders into the high and low goals to score points.  The teams also got points for climbing the tower at the end of the match.

# The Robot
![[IMG_2252.jpg|600]]

The team's robot for that season was named Sir Cyr, after a graduating student from the [[2015 Recycle Rush]] season.  The robot used a pneumatic wheel West Coast Drive to defeat the terrain based defenses, and had an arm to overcome defenses that needed to be interacted with.  The robot could score in both the low and high goals, using vision to align with the high goal.

# Competitions
All match data from [The Blue Alliance](https://www.thebluealliance.com/)

## Greater Toronto Central Regional

The team's first event of 2016 was the Greater Toronto Central competition.  Through 10 qualifying matches, the team went 8-2, ranking 6th.  The team captained alliance 4, and was eliminated in the semifinals.  The team won the [[Team Spirit Award]]
![[IMG_2256.jpg]]
## Windsor Essex Great Lakes Regional

The team's second event of the season was the Windsor Essex competition.  Through 9 qualifying matched, the team went 7-2, ranking 10th.  The team was the first pick of alliance 6, and was eliminated in the quarterfinals.  The team won the [[Innovation in Control Award]]
