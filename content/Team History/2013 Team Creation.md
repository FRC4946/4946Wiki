Our team was formed on September 10th, 2013 by four senior students: Brendan Fallon, Stefan Pleava, Jeremy Sant, and Cole Cyr. The four seniors participated in the University of Toronto’s Robotics competition, which motivated them to start [[FIRST Robotics Competition]] (FRC) Team 4946, The Alpha Dogs, with the guidance of Mr. Torontali and Mr. Ziskos, two then manufacturing teachers at Humberview Secondary School. After growing and organizing the team, we competed as rookies in FIRST [[2014 Aerial Assist]] in January of 2014.
