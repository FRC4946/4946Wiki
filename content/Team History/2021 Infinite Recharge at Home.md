The 2021 [[FIRST Robotics Competition]] season, Infinite Recharge at Home, was the team's eighth season competing.  Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, there were no competitions.  Teams instead competed in 3 categories: Game design, Innovation, and Robot Skills.

# Game Design
In the game design challenge, teams were tasked with coming up with a future game for the [[FIRST Robotics Competition]].  Using provided parameters and game objects, teams had to come up with rules and a game animation for their game.  The team created Pirate Plunder, a pirate themed game.

# Robot Skills
In the robot skills challenge, teams had a number of tasks they had to complete to display the robots capabilities to drive both autonomously and remote driven, and also shoot power cells.  In this challenge, the team won the [[Excellence in Engineering Award]].
