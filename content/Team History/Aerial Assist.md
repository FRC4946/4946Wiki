The 2014 [[FIRST Robotics Competition]], season Aerial Assist was the teams first season competing.  

# The Game
In Aerial Assist, teams passed an alliance coloured ball between their alliance robots and human players, over a truss, and into goals at the ends of the field to score points.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxp4dkMQ1Vo&ab_channel=dblavery)

# The Robot
The team's robot, names Alph was built on a kit bot drivetrain, with an extending intake and 4 shooter wheels to power the ball over the truss and into the high goal.


# Competitions
All match data from [The Blue Alliance](https://www.thebluealliance.com/)
## Greater Toronto West Regional

 We competed in the Greater Toronto West Regional (GTR West) competition, winning the Highest Rookie Seed Award, the Innovation in Control Award, as well as the Rookie Inspiration Award. 

## North Bay Regional

 After our success at the GTR West competition, we decided to compete in the North Bay Regional competition in an attempt to make it to the world championships. At this competition we won three additional awards, including the Rookie All-Star Award, allowing us to go to the FRC World Championships in St. Louis, Missouri. 

## World Championship
 
 We played with great enthusiasm and won the Rookie Inspiration Award on the global stage. We also competed in the FIRST Fall-Fiesta off-season competition, captaining the winning alliance and winning the Fall-Fiesta Best Rookie Team Award.
