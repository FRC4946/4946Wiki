The 2022 [[FIRST Robotics Competition]] season, Rapid React, was the team's ninth season competing.  This season used one day qualifying events due to restrictions from COVID-19.  These events only had 4 alliance playoffs instead of the normal 8.

# The Game
In Rapid React, teams shot cargo into the lower and upper hub.  At the end of the match, teams would climb in their hanger.

# The Robot
![[JuCbyLZ.jpeg|600]]

The team's robot that year was named Curious George due to the design of the climber.  The robot used a Coleson and omni-wheel West Coast Drive, with an over the bumper intake and a flywheel shooter.  The robot also had pneumatic claws on a rotating axle for the climb.

# Competitions
All match data from [The Blue Alliance](https://www.thebluealliance.com/)

## York University District Event - Day 2

The team's first event of the 2022 season was the York University competition.  Through 9 qualification matches, the team went 4-5, seeding 10th.  The team was the first pick of alliance 4, and were eliminated in the semifinals.
![](https://youtu.be/aMdFZ-XQL7k)
## St. Mary District Event - Day 1

The team's second event was the St. Mary Competition.  Through 9 qualification matches, the team went 4-5, seeding 10th.  The team was the 2nd pick of alliance 4, and was eliminated in the semifinals.  The team also won the [[Industrial Design Award]].
![](https://youtu.be/Nq7lwmidENQ)
## Ontario Provincial Championship

Due to low registration in the 2022 [[FIRST Robotics Competition]] season due to the COVID-19 restrictions, all teams in Ontario were invited to the provincial championship.  Through 12 qualification matches, the team went 9-3, seeding 10th.  The team captained alliance 8, and was eliminated in the quarterfinals.  The team also won the [[Excellence in Engineering Award]].  The team ranked 19th in Ontario.
