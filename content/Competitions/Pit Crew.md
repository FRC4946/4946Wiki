The pit crew is the group of team members responsible for the maintenance and repair of the robot at events.  They often consist of members from the [[content/Build/Build|Build]] and [[Programming]] sub-teams, but can be a member of any.  During a competition there are usually 3 pit crew members in the pit at a given time, with at least one being in the pit at all times to ensure that there is a point of contact for the team there, and that nothing is lost or stolen.
