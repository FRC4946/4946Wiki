The FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is the highest level in the [FIRST](https://www.firstinspires.org/) program. FRC was started by [Dean Kamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Kamen) and [Dr. Woodie Flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodie_Flowers) in 1992, with 28 teams in a high school gymnasium in New Hampshire. FRC now has over 3,500 teams in 28 countries around the world, competing in over 180 events.
