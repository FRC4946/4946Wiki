The Falcon 500 was made by VEX and CTRE.  It is a high power brushless motor, used on drivetrains and large mechanisms.

![[Falcon500.png|400]]
# Falcon 500 Wiring
The Falcon 500 gets its power from either a [[Power Distribution Hub]] or [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]] and is connected to the CAN bus from either a [[RoboRIO]], [[RoboRIO2]], or [[CANivore]].
