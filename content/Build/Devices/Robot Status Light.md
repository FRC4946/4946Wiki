The Robot Status Light (often called RSL) indicates to team members and field volunteers the current status of the robot.

![[rslLight.jpeg]]

# Robot Status Light Indications
- Solid On: Robot is powered on and disabled
- Blinking: Robot is powered on and enabled
- Off: Robot off

# Robot Status Light Wiring
The Robot Status Light is wired to the RSL port on the [[RoboRIO]], [[RoboRIO2]], and [[SystemCore]].
