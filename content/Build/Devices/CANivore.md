The CANivore is a USB to high speed CAN network device made by CTRE.  This device allows teams to wire [[Talon FX]] controlled motors, and other CTRE CAN devices onto a separate CAN bus to improve performance.

![[canivore_main__07095_1638900553_1280_1280__43296_1643920037_386_513.jpeg]]

# CANivore Wiring
The CANivore gets powered from a 2 A fused port on a [[Power Distribution Hub]], [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]], or [[Mini Power Module]].  It can also be powered from a 2 A port on a [[Voltage Regulator Module]].  The USB A connector is connected into one of the USB A ports on the [[RoboRIO]], [[RoboRIO2]], or [[SystemCore]].  Yellow and green CAN wires are run from the remaining ports.
