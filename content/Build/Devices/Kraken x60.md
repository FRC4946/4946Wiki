The Kraken x60 is made by West Coast Products and CTRE.  It is a high power brushless motor used on drivetrains and large mechanisms.

![[Kraken x60.jpeg|400]]

# Kraken x60 Wiring
The Kraken x60 gets its power from either a [[Power Distribution Hub]] or [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]] and is connected to the CAN bus from either a [[RoboRIO]], [[RoboRIO2]], or [[CANivore]].
