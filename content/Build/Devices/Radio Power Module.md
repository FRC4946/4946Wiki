The Radio Power Module (also called an RPM) is made by REV.  It takes power from either a [[Power Distribution Hub]] or [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]] and provides a power over ethernet source to a [[VH-109]] or [[OM5P-AC]] radio.

![[RPM.jpeg|400]]

# Radio Power Module Wiring
The Radio Power Module receives power from a small amperage slot on a [[Power Distribution Hub]] or a normal power slot on a [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]].  An ethernet cable connects from the [[RoboRIO]] or [[RoboRIO2]] to the RPM to transfer control signal.  An ethernet cable connects from the Radio Power Module to either a [[VH-109]] or [[OM5P-AC]] robot radio, providing control signal and power over ethernet.
