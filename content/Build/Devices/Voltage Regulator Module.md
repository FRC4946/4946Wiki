The Voltage Regulator Module (often called a VRM) is made by CTRE.  It takes a 12 V input, and gives consistent 5 V and 12 V outputs at 500mA and 2 A for each voltage.

![[VRM.jpeg|400]]

# Voltage Regulator Module Wiring
The Voltage Regulator Module is wired to one of the larger ports on the [[Power Distribution Hub]] or [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]], and provides power to devices like [[CANcoder]]s, [[CANivore]], and cameras.
