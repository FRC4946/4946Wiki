The Power Distribution Panel 2.0 made by CTRE is a main power distribution device for use on [[FIRST Robotics Competition]] robots.

![[PDP2.jpeg|400]]

# Power Distribution Panel 2.0 Hardware Specifications
- Large WAGO Power Ports: 24

## Breaker Sizes
- ATO auto resetting breakers: 10 A, 20 A, 30 A, 40 A

# Power Distribution Panel 2.0 Wiring
The Power Distribution Hub main power supply is connected to the battery and [[120 Amp Breaker]].  All devices are connected to the push-pin connectors.
