The MiniCIM motor is made by CCL Industrial Motor.  It is a intermediate size brushed motor, used for intakes and shooters.

![[MiniCIM.jpeg|400]]

# MiniCIM Wiring
The MiniCIM motor is wired to a [[Talon SRX]] or [[Victor SPX]] controller, providing power to the motor.
