The Mini Power Module (also known as an MPM) is made by REV.  It provides multiple smaller amperage main power connections from one larger source.

![[MPM.png|400]]

# Mini Power Module Wiring
The Mini Power Modules main WAGO is wired to one of the larger ports on the [[Power Distribution Hub]] or [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]].  Fuses are added to each channel used on the device, and then the smaller WAGOs are wired to the devices like an [[Orange Pi]] or [[Limelight]].
