The Krakenx44 is made by West Coast Products and CTRE.  It is a brushless motor, smaller than the [[Kraken x60]], but still provides power for shooters and intakes.

![[Kraken x44.jpeg|400]]

# Kraken x44 Wiring
The Kraken x44 gets its power from either a [[Power Distribution Hub]] or [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]] and is connected to the CAN bus from either a [[RoboRIO]], [[RoboRIO2]], or [[CANivore]].
