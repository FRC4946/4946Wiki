The VH-109 robot radio is made by VividHosting.  The radio connects the robot to the driver station during practice or to the field during competitions.  This radio replaced the [[OM5P-AC]] robot radio in 2025.

![[VH-109.jpeg|400]]

# VH-109 Specifications 
- WI-FI bands: 2.4 GHz practice band, 6GHz competition band
- Power Over Ethernet ports: 2

# VH-109 Wiring
The VH-109 can be powered in a few different ways, but the team generally choses to power it using a [[Radio Power Module]].  The radio power module is attached to a [[Power Distribution Hub]] or [[Power Distribution Panel 2.0]] and powers the radio using power over ethernet.
