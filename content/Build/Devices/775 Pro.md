The 775 Pro motor is made by VEX and West Coast Products.  It's a small package brushed motor, useful for lower power applications.

# 775 Pro Wiring
The 775 Pro is wired to a [[Talon SRX]] and [[Victor SPX]] controller, providing power to the motor.
