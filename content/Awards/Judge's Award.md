The Judges Award is awarded when the judging panel may decide a team’s unique efforts, performance, or dynamics merit recognition.

# Award Guidelines
- The team keeps appearing for consideration for other awards.
- Other Judges have noticed and commented on the positive aspects of the team.
- A unique happening or feature (often one that demonstrates the team has fully embraced the principles of _FIRST_) has caught a Judge’s attention.
# Team Approach
The team works hard to stay in consideration for all awards, and tries to embrace the FIRST core values at all times.
